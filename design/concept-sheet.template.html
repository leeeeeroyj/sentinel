SENTINEL

Hold the ring. Patrol the endless loop of an orbital colony, shoot down the drones that snatch its people, and catch every colonist they drop.

Patrol screen

Live mockup at the game's real resolution (416×228). The ring is 8 screens around and loops, so keep flying and you come back to where you started. It plays a demo until you click the screen and take over. Sprites you pick below swap in here.

The hullCode-drawn, never sprites: domes, towers and a lit strip that repeat every lap of the ring. They go dark when the last colonist is lost.

Your craft & the colonists

The craft flips to face the way you fly, so pick a side-on sprite. Every candidate is drawn nose-left, so Mirror starts on and the ship faces right at rest.

Hostiles

Five roles. Each one shows a warning before it can hurt you: a closing warp ring when it arrives, a white flash before it fires, a lamp before Hounds come.

HUD icon

Shown at half size in the top bar (8 px), still pixel-exact because the game renders at 4×.

How a wave plays

10 colonistsThey walk the hull. Snatchers drop on them and climb away. Reach the top and the Snatcher and its catch fuse into a Husk. That colonist is gone.
Catch 500 · Land 500Shoot a Snatcher that's lifting someone and they fall. Catch them in mid-air for 500 and fly them down to the hull for 500 more. A short fall (under 5 tiles) they survive on their own: 250. Any higher and they're lost.
Groups of fiveSnatchers warp in five at a time: 15 in wave 1, then 3 more each wave. Destroy every Snatcher, Husk and Minelayer to end the wave.
Wave bonus100 × the wave number for each colonist still alive, capped at 500 each from wave 5.
The ring goes darkLose the last colonist and the hull lights die. Every Snatcher turns into a Husk, and new ones arrive as Husks. Colonists and lights return at the start of waves 5, 10, 15 and so on.
HoundsLinger in a wave for 60 s (5 s less each wave, 30 s at least) and the HOUND lamp blinks. Three seconds later a Hound warps in beside you, then another every 10 s until the wave ends.
3 ships · 3 pulses+1 ship and +1 pulse every 10,000 points. After a respawn your craft blinks and can't be hit for 2 s.
8 screens roundThe ring loops with no edges. The view leads ahead of you and swings round when you turn.

From the 1981 original

Sentinel borrows the mechanics of Williams Electronics' Defender. The name, setting, characters and art are new.

Kept
  • A looping world with a long-range scanner
  • Abduction: fuse with the catch, then chase you
  • Catching falling people, and the point values
  • Smart bombs, extra ship every 10,000
  • World destroyed, restored every 5th wave
  • Fast hunters that punish stalling
Changed
  • Orbital ring colony instead of a planet
  • Push the way you want to go: no thrust or reverse buttons
  • No hyperspace; no pods or swarmers
  • Mines can't be shot (a pulse clears them)
  • New names: Snatcher, Husk, Minelayer, Hound
Added
  • A warning before every hazard can hurt you
  • Edge arrows and a TAKEN lamp for abductions
  • Controller and touch from day one
  • Sparks, shockwaves, shake and procedural sound
